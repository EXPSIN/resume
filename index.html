简介About

吴思，东北大学流程工业综合自动化全国重点实验室博士后、特聘副研究员，合作导师为柴天佑院士。2025 年获东北大学控制科学与工程博士学位，此前于东北大学获控制工程硕士学位（2020）与河南理工大学自动化专业学士学位（2017）。研究方向为非线性系统稳定性、安全攸关控制与约束满足控制，重点关注约束满足控制中的可行性、连续性与鲁棒性问题，提出了可行集重塑方法，并围绕非线性小增益分析、构造性安全攸关控制等方向开展研究。相关成果发表于 IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control、Automatica、IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters 等期刊，并在 IEEE CDC、IROS、ECC 等会议报告。

I am a postdoctoral researcher and Distinguished Associate Research Fellow at the State Key Laboratory of Synthetical Automation for Process Industries, Northeastern University, working with Academician Tianyou Chai. I received the Ph.D. degree in Control Science and Engineering from Northeastern University in 2025, after earning the M.E. degree in Control Engineering (2020) and the B.E. degree in Automation (2017). My research concerns the feasibility, continuity, and robustness issues in constraint-satisfaction control; I proposed a feasible-set reshaping method and have been working on nonlinear small-gain analysis and constructive safety-critical control. Results have appeared in IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, Automatica, and IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, and were presented at IEEE CDC, IROS, and ECC.
